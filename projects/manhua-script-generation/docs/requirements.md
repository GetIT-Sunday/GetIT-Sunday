# Requirements (Sanitized)

## Product Need

Create a repeatable novel-to-manhua-script workflow that reduces dependence on manual adaptation while preserving the narrative information required by downstream production.

## Inputs

- Novel chapters or other authorized source text
- Character and world-setting information when available
- Production format and episode constraints

## Outputs

- Story outline
- Character profiles for protagonists, key supporting characters, and antagonists
- Episode-level outline with plot, hook, emotional anchor, and ending point
- Formatted episode script with scene and visual directions

## Acceptance Criteria

- Core source information is retained and traceable through the planning stages.
- Episode boundaries and pacing are coherent.
- Characters, relationships, events, and world settings remain consistent.
- Output follows the agreed production format.
- The result can enter human production review without extensive structural rewriting.
