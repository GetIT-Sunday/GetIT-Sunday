# Evaluation Design (Sanitized)

## Evaluation Layers

- **Expert review:** final quality judgment for narrative appeal and production usability.
- **Rubric scoring:** repeatable checks for source adherence, pacing, language, visual instructions, and formatting.
- **Model-based checks:** automated detection of missing elements, contradictions, rhythm deviations, and formatting errors.

## Evaluation Targets

### Extraction

- Were major and minor plot points identified?
- Were hooks, reversals, emotional beats, and high-impact lines retained?
- Are character motivations and relationships represented correctly?

### Episode Planning

- Is the episode boundary reasonable?
- Does the episode contain a clear hook and progression?
- Are emotional beats and turning points distributed effectively?

### Script Generation

- Does the body follow the episode outline?
- Are source highlights and character states preserved?
- Are scene headers, action, dialogue, subtitles, voice-over, flashbacks, and camera cues formatted consistently?
